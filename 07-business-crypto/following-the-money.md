# Following the Money

Financial open sources go far beyond company registries. Property, courts,
nonprofits, government spending — wealth leaves paper trails everywhere.

## Real estate

US county records are shockingly open:

- **County assessor/recorder websites** — deeds, sale prices, mortgage amounts,
  liens, sometimes floor plans. Free, searchable by owner name in most counties.
  Find the county via address → search owner name → find their OTHER properties.
- **NYC ACRIS** — all city property transactions, free.
- **UK Land Registry** — £7 per title document; ownership + price paid.
- Zillow/Redfin history adds listing photos over time — see inside homes without
  entering them (use lawfully).

## Aviation & vessels as wealth markers

- **FAA Registry** — every US-registered aircraft's owner, free, by tail number OR
  owner name. Trust-fund jets, corporate shells owning planes — all listed.
- **MarineTraffic/VesselFinder** — yacht positions live; hull registration lookups
  give ownership chains through holding companies.

## Nonprofits & foundations

- **ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer** — every US nonprofit's IRS Form 990: salaries of
  top staff, grants paid, board members. Free full-text search. Family foundations
  quietly funding causes are mapped here in detail.
- **Candid/GuideStar** — broader nonprofit profiles.

## Government money

- **USAspending.gov** — every federal contract/grant: who won, how much, what for.
- **TED (ted.europa.eu)** — EU tender awards similarly.
- Follow procurement: company wins contract → contract value funds the mansion the
  county recorder just recorded. Chains like this build themselves.

## Courts & debt

Bankruptcies, judgments, liens — PACER for federal, state portals elsewhere. A
subject's litigation history reveals business partners, disputes, and settlements
they'd rather forget. Divorce filings especially leak financial detail.

## Sanctions/PEP screening

Covered in company-records.md via OpenSanctions — but remember adverse media
searching too: `"[name]" (fraud OR investigation OR indicted OR sued)` across news
archives (GDELT, Google News date-filtered).

## The mindset

Money investigations are chain-building: person → property → mortgage lender →
related entity → new person. Every document names parties; every party gets searched;
every search yields documents. Tedious, legal, devastatingly effective.

## The line

All above sources are official/public. Buying stolen financial records, bank
statement dumps, or "fullz" packages from criminal markets is not OSINT — it's
possession of stolen goods with extra steps. The legitimate path is slower and it
holds up in court.
